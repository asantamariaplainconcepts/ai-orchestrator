Explain the area of this repository that the story you have been given concerns, to somebody who
knows the language but has never read this codebase.

Read before you write. Everything you say must be traceable to a file you actually opened, and you
should cite paths as you go — `src/thing/Handler.cs:41` rather than "the handler".

Cover:

- **What it does**, in one paragraph.
- **How the pieces connect** — the path a request, event or call takes through it, in order.
- **What surprised you.** The thing a newcomer would get wrong: a name that means something
  unexpected, an ordering that matters, a check that looks redundant and is not.
- **What you could not work out.** Say so explicitly instead of filling the gap with a plausible
  guess. An honest gap is useful; an invented explanation costs somebody a day.

Do not modify any file.
